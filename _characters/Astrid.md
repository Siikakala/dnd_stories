Backstory:
Tall and striking, with golden blond hair framing piercing grey eyes, **Astrid the Unbound** carries a magnetic presence and a deep, intuitive wisdom that commands quiet respect even in the darkest domains. Once a wandering Norse _völva_, she was snatched from ancient Midgard when the Mists of Ravenloft swallowed her mid-ritual. Where her subtle _seiðr_ magic was once a modest spark in the mortal realm, the heavy supernatural aura of this Land of Dread transformed it into a roaring fire, forging her into a seasoned survivor.

When Astrid strikes her **witch drum** and sways in rhythmic ritual, the veil thins, drawing restless spirits into her circle. Through these trances, she caught vague, cryptic visions of the unlikely companions destined to cross her path—shadowy outlines of twin minds that warp reality, a dark caster carrying the faint scent of a distant city of iron and stone, a devout priest from misty lochs, and a stoic, silver-haired monster hunter. Her spiritual eyes can occasionally pierce the deepest illusions to see unseen entities as they truly are, while an ancient talisman at her neck smooths her words, allowing her to converse effortlessly with travelers from any realm.

In combat, she relies on her heavy, rune-etched **völvustaffr** to strike down dark beasts, channeling its lingering magic to cleanse poisons, lift curses, and knit together deadly wounds.

Unlike most souls trapped within the fog, **Astrid has no desire to return to her old world**. Back in Midgard, her craft was exhausting and faint; here, her connection to the spiritual realm makes her formidable. Driven to shelter others from the domain's horrors, she chooses to stay—using her formidable power to protect her allies while interrogating the dead to uncover the ultimate mystery: the true, hidden nature of the Mists themselves.
