# Episode 1: Session 0 &amp; Trouble in Ashabenford

## Overview &amp; Table Vibe

Welcome to the first deep-dive episode of the Dalelands Campaign! In this session, Dungeon Master Muhrri and players Siika (Quni), Jenni (Jackson), Calzu (Jason), Haukka (Kevan/Kadak), Tsuru (Fendithas), and Smoggis (Wölgarr) set the stage in DR 1372\. The session introduces table dynamics, including house rules like the *säkäheitto* (luck roll), the table's signature 'Finglish' blend of Finnish roleplay with English 3.0e mechanics, and key rules differences between D&amp;D 3.0e and 5e (skill points, saving throws, and armor class calculations).

## Key Narrative Beats

* **Formation at the White Hart Inn**: The party forms in Ashabenford with modest mercenary ambitions.
* **The Drained Pond &amp; Metallic Cube**: Investigating a mysteriously drained pond leads the group to discover a strange metallic cube.
* **Captain Belran's Horse**: Tracking down stolen property from local bandits.
* **Rescuing Lord Galath**: Uncovering Erven's betrayal, Bane cultist activity, and defeating Multhimmer's son Draff to rescue Lord Galath.

## 3.0e Rules &amp; Tactical Mechanics

* **Säkäheitto (Luck Roll)**: A opposing d20 roll between DM and player used for creative actions and random encounter severity.
* **Skill Systems &amp; Saving Throws**: Explaining Fortitude, Reflex, and Will saves alongside skill point allocation in 3.0e compared to 5e's Proficiency bonus system.

## Character Spotlights

* **Quni**: Halfling druid riding her wolf companion Nyx.
* **Jackson**: Farm-boy fighter focused on defending his family and homeland.
* **Jason**: Tactical rogue with an eye for trade and gold.
