# Episode 5: The Elf Vault &amp; Fastren's Fall

## Overview &amp; Table Vibe

Combining ancient elven riddles with planar raids into ethereal pocket dimensions.

## Key Narrative Beats

* **Standing Stone Vault**: Solving Corellon riddles while fighting minotaurs, animated statues, and mimics.
* **Fastren's Tragedy**: Discovering that Holfast's former adventuring companion Fastren succumbed to corruption and became a lich.
* **Ethereal Plane Raid**: Conducting a planar assault into an Ethereal pocket dimension to destroy Fastren and his phylactery.

## 3.0e Rules &amp; Tactical Mechanics

* **Item Hardness &amp; Sunder Rules**: How object hardness and hit points work in 3.0e when targeting magical focus items or phylacteries.
* **Planar Travel Penalties**: Rules governing combat on the Ethereal Plane.

## Character Spotlights

* **Zelene**: Joining the party as a tiefling sorceress devoted to Selûne, bringing immense firepower.
