Welcome back to the campaign companion blog! Episode 7 marks a pivotal transition in the Dalelands campaign. As our Level 10+ heroes leave Ashabenford to address a Kingdom-wide trade collapse across Cormyr, their journey takes them from Far Realm planar anomalies in Tilverton to high-stakes realpolitik in Suzail, culminating in a deadly dungeon delve into the ancient Warrior’s Crypt (*Soturin krypta*).

Drawing directly from campaign records across mid-2020 (`Campaign notes 2020-05-27`, `2020-06-02`, `2020-06-08`, and `2020-06-17` alongside session recordings `2020-06-02-dalelands.mp3`, `2020-06-08-dalelands.mp3`, and `2020-06-17-dalelands.mp3`), this breakdown examines the exact events, tactical battles, and heartbreaking character loss that defined this chapter.

---

## 1\. Setting Out from Ashabenford: The Heartland Trade Collapse (1 Hammer DR 1372)

As recorded in `Campaign notes 2020-05-27`, the party's downtime in Ashabenford was interrupted when **Morngrim Amkhasra** of Shadowdale and **Haresk** arrived with grave intelligence:

* **Heartland Trade Blockades**: Major trade arteries connecting Cormyr, Sembia, and Baldur's Gate had completely ground to a halt due to military blockades, rogue Zhentarim activity, and unnatural Far Realm interference.
* **Call from Suzail**: Steel Regent **Alusair Obarskyr** was actively summoning veteran adventurers to the royal court in Suzail to help restore stability. Recognizing that Ashabenford's long-term security depended on Cormyr's economy, the party prepared to march south via Tilverton on 6 Hammer.

---

## 2\. The Tilverton Anomaly &amp; Commander Ricter's Deception (8 Hammer)

Reaching the ruined crossroads of Tilverton on **8 Hammer**, the party met **Commander Ricter** of the Purple Dragon Knights (`Campaign notes 2020-06-02`). Ricter directed the party toward a dark, corrupted stone well in the town square that was spewing Far Realm shadow-stuff (*varjoaines*).

### The Divine Combo &amp; The False Commander

* **Purging the Well**: Investigating the well, Zelene cast *Limited Wish* to amplify Wölgarr's *Turn Undead*, joining Kevan's Sune turning and Wölgarr's radiant laser burst from Sargathuld. The combined divine burst completely vaporized the shadow portal.
* **Kevan's Lie**: To prevent Cormyrian authorities from attempting to study or weaponize Far Realm residual energy, Kevan informed Commander Ricter that the anomaly had been completely erased.
* **The "Ricter" Ambush**: Upon delivering the report, "Ricter" and his honor guards suddenly drew weapons, attacking Kevan during a brief mind-freeze blackout before vanishing into thin air! Interrogating the remaining guards revealed that the real Commander Ricter had departed for Suzail two days prior—the entity blockading Tilverton was an outer-planar Far Realm projection mimicking Ricter's form.

---

## 3\. High Suzail Realpolitik &amp; The Stonelands Keep Grant

Arriving in Suzail on **16 Hammer**, Kevan leveraged his noble merchant family connections (**the Galadnee family**) to secure an immediate royal audience with Steel Regent **Alusair Obarskyr** and **General Mangerdas** (`Campaign notes 2020-06-08`):

1. **Cormyrian Sponsorship**: The court granted the party official Cormyrian backing and sole territorial authority over the wild **Stonelands** region to construct their own stronghold (**Stonelands Keep**).
2. **The Wards of Warrior's Crypt**: To ensure their new keep could not be targeted by Zhentarim scrying or Far Realm eavesdropping, Alusair and Galadnee advised the party to explore the nearby **Warrior's Crypt** (*Soturin krypta*). The ancient crypt was known to contain powerful, ancient anti-scrying and anti-eavesdropping wards (*vakoilua estävä magia*).

---

## 4\. Into the Warrior's Crypt: Zhent Skirmish &amp; The Four Pits (17 Hammer)

Marching through Eveningstar with local guide Rangnir, the party located the hidden entrance to the Warrior's Crypt on **17 Hammer** (`Campaign notes 2020-06-17`).

### Clearing the Zhentarim Outpost

Breaching the upper level, the party surprised an entrenched squad of Zhentarim mercenaries who had established a staging base inside the crypt. Combat was swift and decisive—the party wiped out the Zhent defenders and looted their quarters before advancing deeper.

### The Central Chamber: 4 Hatches and 4 Pits

Descending into the crypt's central crossroads, the adventurers discovered a complex layout:

* **The Central Pedestal**: A massive stone block in the center of the room with four sealed hatches (one on each side).
* **The Four Hallways**: Four branching corridors led away from the pedestal, each ending in a deep pit in the floor:
&nbsp;&nbsp;1. **Pit 1 (Ethereal Blades)**: Hovering above the pit were invisible, swirling force blades (a *Rage Wind* spirit trap).
&nbsp;&nbsp;2. **Pit 2 (Crushed Corpses)**: Surrounded by the mangled, crushed remains of previous intruders.
&nbsp;&nbsp;3. **Pit 3 (Red Mist)**: Covered by a thick, unnatural crimson mist.
&nbsp;&nbsp;4. **Pit 4 (The Dormant Golem)**: Containing a massive, silent metallic construct.

### Wölgarr's Tobacco Vision

To determine what lay inside the four sealed hatches without triggering traps, Wölgarr smoked his magical tobacco, allowing his sight to shift onto the Ethereal Plane (`Campaign notes 2020-06-17`). On the Ethereal Plane, Wölgarr saw that each hatch contained a specific magical item, connected by glowing ethereal ribbons directly to its corresponding corridor pit:

* **Hatch 1**: A white cylindrical rod (connected to Pit 4).
* **Hatch 2**: 10 magic blueberries.
* **Hatch 3**: A Vorpal Dagger.
* **Hatch 4**: A Shadowstaff.

---

## 5\. Activating the Mithril Golem &amp; The Death of Dush

Reaching into Hatch 1, Wölgarr pulled out the white rod. Instantly, the ethereal connection severed, animating the **Mithril Golem** in Pit 4 (`Campaign notes 2020-06-17`)!

### The Battle &amp; Tactical Retreat

Rising out of its pit, the massive Mithril Golem—a formidable 3.0e construct endowed with innate *Haste* and **Damage Reduction 15/adamantine**—stood directly between the pedestal and the exit doorway.

* **Dush's Frenzy**: Dush immediately entered a **Frenzy** (Frenzied Berserker) and charged the construct with his greataxe. Despite his wild strength, his blows merely scratched the golem's adamantine-grade plating.
* **Spells Bounce Off**: Zelene cast *Thunder Spear*, but her spell bounced uselessly off the construct's innate magic immunity and sky-high Armor Class.
* **Jackson's Order**: Seeing that their attacks were dealing negligible damage while the golem's heavy strikes threatened to obliterate the party, Jackson made the executive tactical call: *"We can't beat this thing—fall back!"* Jackson grabbed Kevan and sprinted for the exit, while Zelene cast *Dimension Door* to bypass the choke point.

### The Tragedy of Dush

Under D&amp;D 3.0e Frenzied Berserker rules, a character in a Frenzy **cannot voluntarily retreat or cease attacking** while enemies remain in sight, unless they succeed on a DC 20 Will save at the end of their turn.

* Trapped in his unstoppable bloodlust, Dush failed his Will save to break out of Frenzy.
* As the rest of the party retreated up the corridor, Dush stood his ground alone against the Mithril Golem.
* The golem battered the barbarian with relentless, haste-boosted slam attacks, pounding Dush into a pulp. **Dush died on the cold stone floor (†)** (`Campaign notes 2020-06-17`).

Escaping the crypt with the anti-scrying artifacts but mourning their fallen comrade, the survivors returned to Eveningstar to regroup.
