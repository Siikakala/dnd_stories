Welcome back to the campaign companion blog! Following the temporary defeat of the Lich in Episode 4, our party's descent into the subterranean catacombs beneath the Standing Stone reaches its dramatic, heartwarming and heartbreaking climax in Episode 5\. Covering campaign sessions from late March through mid-April 2020 (`Campaign notes 2020-03-31`, `2020-04-08`, and `2020-04-16`), this episode delivers some of the heaviest character losses, emotional choices, and high-level planar combat of our entire early campaign.

From tragic wilderness casualties and nature-magic reincarnations to unmasking tavern secrets and assaulting an undead stronghold on the Ethereal Plane, here is the complete chronological breakdown for Episode 5.

---

## 1\. Catastrophe Beneath the Standing Stone: The Earth Elemental Ambush

As recorded in `Campaign notes 2020-03-31`, the party pushed deeper into the lower catacombs beneath the Standing Stone. Exploring the unmapped corridors, they discovered a heavy, sealed stone portal requiring a forgotten password. Choosing to search adjacent side-chambers for clues before attempting to breach the door, the adventurers walked directly into a devastating ambush.

A massive, primeval **Earth Elemental** erupted from the cavern floor, catching the party completely off guard in tight quarters:

* **Frontline Collapse**: The construct-like force of the Earth Elemental immediately slammed into Dush, beating the barbarian to the ground.
* **Fatal Blows**: Turning its crushing blows onto the rest of the party, the Earth Elemental dealt catastrophic bludgeoning damage—slaying **Jackson** on the spot (†) and crushing Quni's animal companion, the wolf **Nyx**, to death (†).
* **Quni's Reincarnation Magic**: Desperate to save her companion, Quni channeled her primal druidic magic to bring Jackson back from death, restoring him to life as a human warrior.

---

## 2\. The Corrupted Treant &amp; Quni's Tragic End

Stunned by the loss of Nyx and Jackson's near-death, the surviving party members pressed forward into an expansive subterranean grove chamber (`Campaign notes 2020-03-31`). Looming in the center of the chamber was a giant, blighted **Treant**, corrupted by the necrotic energies radiating from the dungeon's ancient magic.

The party rallied to cut down the Treant. Upon its death, the construct-like plant creature released a pool of glowing, magical sap. Heartbroken and overwhelmed with grief over the sudden death of her wolf Nyx, **Quni** made a desperate, emotional decision—choosing to take a deadly gamble by drinking the unknown magical sap.

The concentrated magical reaction proved instantly fatal, killing **Quni** on the spot (†). In a single brutal session, the party suffered the loss of both Nyx and Quni, marking one of the darkest turns in the Dalelands campaign.

---

## 3\. Surfacing to Bloodshed: The Orc Ambush &amp; Zelene's Arrival

Emerging battered, mourning, and exhausted from the Standing Stone catacombs on **8 April 2020** (`Campaign notes 2020-04-08`), the survivors stepped out into the crisp Dalelands air only to find the surface surrounded by a warband of **Orcs** waiting in ambush. Despite their grief and depleted spell slots, Jackson, Dush, Wölgarr, Kevan, and Jason fought with savage focus, annihilating the orc ambushers to the last warrior.

### Zelene Joins the Party

While marching back along the forest road toward Ashabenford, the heroes encountered **Zelene** (played by Siika), a Tiefling Sorceress devoted to Selûne.

Following Quni's tragic death, Siika introduced Zelene to the campaign—a character with a complex backstory involving a human mother who loved her despite her fiendish bloodline. Zelene’s arcane sorcery and devotion to eradicate evil provided an immediate magic boost to a party reeling from recent losses.

---

## 4\. The Betrayal of Holdfast: Interrogating Innkeeper Holfast

Returning to Ashabenford on **8 April 2020**, the party headed directly to Holfast's Inn to confront the innkeeper **Holfast** (`Campaign notes 2020-04-08`). Having discovered ancient clues connecting Holfast to the Standing Stone dungeon, the party pressed him for the truth behind the Lich lurking beneath the monument.

Under intense interrogation, Holfast broke down and confessed the tragic history of his former adventuring party, originally named **Holdfast**:

* Years ago, Holfast and his companion **Fastren** delved into the Standing Stone ruins.
* Deep inside the catacombs, the group was ambushed by a swarm of giant wasps. Panicking, Holfast fled the dungeon to save himself, locking the doors behind him and abandoning Fastren to the swarm.
* Trapped in the dark, Fastren survived the wasps but succumbed to necrotic corruption, eventually transforming himself into a vengeful **Lich** who nursed a bitter hatred for Holfast and the living world.

---

## 5\. Planar Assault: Slaying the Lich Fastren on the Ethereal Plane

With Fastren's physical body temporarily destroyed in Episode 4, the party learned that his spirit and **phylactery** were hidden within a personal pocket realm anchored to the **Ethereal Plane** (`Campaign notes 2020-04-16`). To end Fastren's threat permanently, the party prepared for interplanar war.

On **16 April 2020**, after casting extensive protective buffs, the party teleported onto the Ethereal Plane:

1. **The Ethereal Standing Stone**: The adventurers materialized at an ethereal reflection of the Standing Stone, where Fastren had bound planar guardians. The party was immediately attacked by a massive, kraken-like **Water Elemental** flanked by planar sharks. Combining Jackson's martial power with Zelene's sorcery, the party dispatched the aquatic beasts.
2. **The Corridor Guardian**: Pushing into the ethereal catacombs, the heroes fought and destroyed a hulking demonic guardian guarding the inner sanctum.
3. **Phylactery Destruction**: Reaching Fastren's inner chamber, the party engaged the Lich in a final, spell-slinging battle. Slaying Fastren's ethereal form, Wölgarr and Jason located his phylactery and shattered it completely, wiping Fastren from existence once and for all!

### Return to Noristuor's Cellar

With the phylactery destroyed, the party cast planar teleportation magic to return safely to **Noristuor's cellar** in Ashabenford, concluding the Standing Stone dungeon arc.

---

## 6\. Looking Ahead: The Road to Shadowdale (Episode 6 Hook)

Although the Lich Fastren has been erased from Faerûn, the victory came at a staggering cost. The brutal planar assault and previous subterranean battles left **Dush** and **Wölgarr** fallen, requiring high-level divine intervention to bring them back.

As Episode 5 ends, the party gathers in Ashabenford to prepare for their next leg: a **3-day travel march to Shadowdale** (`Campaign notes 2020-04-20`) to seek out **High Cleric Tearan** at the House of the Morning, setting the stage for Episode 6!
