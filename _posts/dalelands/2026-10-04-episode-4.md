# Episode 4: Reclaiming Tethyamar

## Overview &amp; Table Vibe

A classic Underdark dungeon crawl packed with subterranean threats and dwarven ancestral honor.

## Key Narrative Beats

* **Underdark Descent**: Navigating treacherous vertical shafts while fending off bulettes and a hostile Feyri.
* **Tethyamar's Workshop**: Infiltrating the lost dwarven mines to locate Fyrfar Smokebeard's notes, Borlin's skeleton, and the *Sarghathuld* blade.
* **Vault Showdown**: Defeating Kara Chermos and Arvien in the main vault to free enslaved dwarven miners.

## 3.0e Rules &amp; Tactical Mechanics

* **Possession Mechanics**: Rules for handling fiendish and shade possession during combat.
* **Subterranean Combat &amp; Verticality**: Managing movement, sight, and spell ranges in deep Underdark shafts.

## Character Spotlights

* **Wölgarr**: Clothed in dwarven reverence as he recovers his father Borlin's remains and ancestral legacy.
