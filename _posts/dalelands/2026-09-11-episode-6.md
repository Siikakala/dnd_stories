Welcome back to the campaign companion blog! Episode 6 marks a dramatic shift in scale for our Dalelands campaign. Having survived the tragic catacombs beneath the Standing Stone in Episode 5—where Quni met her bittersweet end drinking the corrupted treant sap, making way for **Zelene** (played by Siika), the Tiefling Sorceress of Selûne—our adventurers embark on a high-flying, epic-tier expedition across Faerûn.

Drawing directly from session notes (`Campaign notes 2020-04-20`, `2020-04-27`, `2020-05-07`, `2020-05-13`) and the very first live audio session recordings (`mono-2020-05-13`, `mono-2020-05-18`, `mono-2020-05-27`), this companion breakdown walks through the full narrative arc of Episode 6.

---

## 1\. The Shadowdale Resurrections &amp; The Yule Santa Wishes

The episode opens on **20 Nightal DR 1372**. Carrying the corpses of Wölgarr and Dush across the snow from the Ethereal Plane rift back to Shadowdale, the surviving party—Jackson, Jason, Kevan, and Zelene—seek out the high cleric **Tearan**. Through divine grace, Tearan successfully casts *Raise Dead*, bringing both Wölgarr and Dush back to life.

### Decimating Nuthbok's Stronghold (21 Nightal)

Reunited, the party moves to eliminate **Lord Nuthbok**, a bandit warlord operating out of a fortified stronghold near Daggerdale. The party breaches the complex, carving through bandit guards, drow spellcasters, an illithid, and an ogre mage before Jackson slays Nuthbok himself.

### The Yule Santa Encounter &amp; Three Wishes

Upon Nuthbok's death, an incredible planar anomaly occurs: a mysterious figure named **Sal'kin**, dressed in a bright red suit, manifests directly from Nuthbok's corpse. Celebrating Yule (21 Nightal), Sal'kin grants the party three extraordinary wishes:

1. **Roldilar Coordinates**: Wölgarr receives the exact geographical coordinates and ancient dwarven lore required to locate the lost kingdom of **Roldilar**.
2. **The Flying Folding Boat**: Jackson's magical *Folding Boat* receives a permanent enchantment, granting it full aerial flight speed.
3. **Vitality Boost**: Every party member receives a permanent hit point increase to prepare for the epic challenges ahead.

---

## 2\. Soaring to Sarbreen and Mount Grimmerfang

Following 10 days of Yule downtime in Ashabenford and Shadowdale, the party boards their newly enchanted flying ship in May 2020 (`Campaign notes 2020-05-07`).

### The Sewer Ruins of Sarbreen

Flying over the Vast to **Raven's Bluff**, the party descends into the subterranean ruins of **Sarbreen** buried beneath the city sewers. Here they recover crucial historical artifacts:

* **Balenwick's Treatise on the Mythal**: An ancient dwarven spellbook detailing how the legendary archmage Balenwick constructed a protective Mythal over Mount Grimmerfang.
* **Borlin's Expedition Journal**: Notes written by Wölgarr's late father, confirming that Master Smith **Fyrfar Smokebeard** had established an epic adamantine forge inside Grimmerfang.

---

## 3\. Master Fyrfar's Forge &amp; Slaying Iyrauroth

Arriving at **Mount Grimmerfang** (`mono-2020-05-13`), the heroes navigate volcanic caverns past rust monsters and ancient traps, discovering three legendary dwarven heroes held in temporal stasis: **Bim**, **Cordozar**, and **Assock**.

### Reforging Sargathuld

In the heart of the mountain, Wölgarr meets **Master Fyrfar Smokebeard** (Thorsamson), who has transcended mortality as a dwarven forge-spirit. Placing **Sargathuld** and a rare **Glowstone** onto his million-gold-piece adamantine anvil, Fyrfar hammers radiant solar energy directly into the blade:

* **Laser Burst Upgrade**: Sargathuld gains a +6d6 radiant laser burst on critical hits and weapon strikes.
* **Fyrfar's Blessing**: Fyrfar channels green divine energy into the party, bestowing the mechanical benefits of a full Long Rest before the dragon siege.

### The Battle of the Volcanic Caldera

Descending into Grimmerfang's lowest caldera (`mono-2020-05-18`), the party engages the green-glowing dragon cultists and two mature black dragon offspring before the main terror emerges: **Iyrauroth**, a colossal, four-headed mutant black dragon (CR 23 boss fight).

Zelene channels high-circle *Chain Lightning*, Jackson and Dush charge into melee, Kevan provides protective auras, and Wölgarr delivers devastating radiant strikes with the newly empowered Sargathuld. Defeating Iyrauroth, the heroes claim an astounding **540,000 gp dragon hoard**, shoveling metric tons of gold, platinum, and ancient artifacts onto the flying Folding Boat (`mono-2020-05-27`).

---

## 4\. The Return Flight: Ambush by the Dragon Turtle

Loaded to maximum capacity with 540,000 gp of dragon treasure, the flying Folding Boat makes its return voyage toward Ashabenford (`2020-05-27` audio transcript). As the ship lowers near the waters of the **Dragon Mere / River Ashaba** on 6 Hammer, a massive shadow surges from the depths—an **Advanced Dragon Turtle** breaches the surface to attack!

### A High-Level Tactical Masterclass

In Episode 2, a simple river Sea Lion nearly wiped out the low-level party. But now, at higher levels, the party displays their growth:

* **Instant Shutdown**: Kevan and Zelene coordinate, casting 3.0e **Hold Monster** on the aquatic beast. The Dragon Turtle fails its save, freezing paralyzed in the water for 18 full rounds!
* **Decimating the Beast**: Jackson, Dush, and Wölgarr leap onto the paralyzed creature's shell, laying into it with heavy full-attack actions and smites until the river turns crimson.
* **Safe Landing**: The party clears the threat in seconds without taking a single point of damage, triumphantly arriving in Ashabenford to unload their legendary fortune into Jason's shop and prepare for the next campaign arc.

## 5\. Campaign Milestone: Transition to Live Audio (May 2020)

Episode 6 marks an incredible milestone in the campaign's real-world history:

* **From Written Notes to Live Transcripts**: Starting with the May 2020 sessions (`2020-05-13`), the campaign shifted from DM session summaries to full live audio recordings over Roll20/Discord.
* **Table Dynamics**: Listeners get to hear the real-time Finglish banter between DM Muhrri and players Siika, Jenni, Calzu, Tsuru, Haukka, and Smoggis—complete with retro CRPG references, rulebook checks, and legendary dice rolls!
