# Episode 2: Shadows over Cormanthor

## Overview &amp; Table Vibe

As the group ventures into Cormanthor, the stakes escalate from local banditry to ancient dwarven legends and Zhentarim espionage.

## Key Narrative Beats

* **Exposing Arvien**: Unmasking the Zhentarim spy Arvien after analyzing a dwarven statue.
* **The Legend of Sarghathuld**: Learning about Fyrfar Smokebeard's legendary dwarven blade designed to slay orcs.
* **Spiderhaunt Woods**: Braving the forest to raid the lair of the green dragon Urna, recovering Borlin's sword and ancient psionic relics.
* **Barrow Fields Disaster**: Noristuor's ritual goes horribly wrong, summoning a deadly flesh golem and ghouls.

## 3.0e Rules &amp; Tactical Mechanics

* **Reincarnation Rules**: In 3.0e, *Reincarnate* rolls on a wide table. Fendithas is reincarnated as a wild boar and Kadak as a gnome before both head into the wilderness.
* **Save-or-Suck &amp; Damage Reduction**: How early 3.0e DR and lethal save-or-die spells create high tension.

## Character Spotlights

* **Noristuor**: The village wizard whose dangerous arcane experiments push the party into chaotic battles.
* **Fendithas &amp; Kadak**: Experiencing the wild realities of early 3.0e character death and reincarnation.
