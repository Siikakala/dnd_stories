Welcome back to the Dalelands Campaign companion blog! Episode 2 takes our level 2 party from regional fixers to true wilderness adventurers. While Episode 1 established the local stakes in Ashabenford, Episode 2 delivers a roller-coaster narrative: translating ancient dwarven lore, hunting aquatic predators, negotiating with planar entities, surviving a horrific midnight massacre at an ancient burial ground, and dealing with the wild, unpredictable consequences of 3.0e *Reincarnate* magic!

This companion post breaks down all the campaign events from late 2019 through January 2020, offering 5e players a deep dive into the gritty 3.0e mechanics that defined these early sessions.

---

## 1\. Deciphering the Runes &amp; The Three Beast Contracts

Following the discovery of the dwarven statuette in Haresk and Holfast's wooden box, the scholar **Arvien** spent days copying and translating its ancient inscriptions. Her work revealed the history of **Fyrfar Smokebeard**, a master dwarven smith who forged **Sargathuld**—a legendary blade crafted specifically to slay orcs. Sargathuld was last wielded by the adventurer group *Grimblade* before vanishing into the monster-infested depths of Cormanthor.

Seeking passage toward the lost **Mines of Tethyamar** where further clues lay, the party met with **Noristuor**, an enigmatic local broker holding court at Holfast's Inn in Ashabenford. Noristuor agreed to assist them, but demanded payment in raw magical components: specifically, the **eye of a magical beast**. He offered three potential targets across the region:

1. **A River Sea Lion** lurking in the local waterways.
2. **An Orc Shaman of Gruumsh** operating near the Standing Stone, two days to the north.
3. **A Ravid** (a positive energy planar serpent) inhabiting an abandoned wizard's tower to the south.

### The River Sea Lion Hunt

The party chose to hunt the river Sea Lion first. Luring the beast to the riverbank with meat enhanced by a local gnome alchemist, the adventurers sprung a heavy net trap. The battle was fast and terrifying—a 3.0e Sea Lion possesses a fearsome Rend ability and heavy natural armor. The beast lunged out of the water, ambushing Quni and dragging her toward the depths while mauling Kadak on the shore. However, through quick coordination from Jackson, Wölgarr, and Jason, the party slew the beast and harvested its eyes for Noristuor.

---

## 2\. The Ravid &amp; The Residuum Bargain

Upon receiving the Sea Lion's eyes, Noristuor revealed his second condition: he required refined **magical residuum** by 29 Marpenoth.

To secure the residuum without a bloody fight, the party traveled south to the abandoned wizard's tower. The surrounding courtyard was guarded by animated furniture brought to life by the Ravid's passive positive energy aura. Rather than drawing weapons, **Fendithas** stepped forward. Utilizing 3.0e diplomacy mechanics and planar lore, Fendithas successfully communicated with the planar serpent, convincing it to yield the required residuum peacefully.

With the residuum delivered to Noristuor on 30 Marpenoth, the broker presented his final term: the party had to escort him to the **Barrow Fields** (an ancient burial ground two days away) to perform a binding ritual.

---

## 3\. The Barrow Fields Massacre: Meat Golems &amp; The Death of Kadak and Fendithas

The party spent two days gearing up in Ashabenford and marching across the autumn wilderness toward the Barrow Fields. On the morning of **3 Uktar**, the party reached the ancient burial grounds. Noristuor began his complex ritual, which was scheduled to conclude at midnight.

As midnight struck and the ritual reached its climax, dark magic surged across the burial mounds. Ghouls, shadows, and a massive, stitched-together **Meat/Flesh Golem** burst from the earth, surrounding the party and Noristuor!

### The Battle at Midnight

Low-level 3.0e combat against a Flesh Golem is notoriously deadly due to its Damage Reduction (DR 10/+1 in 3.0e, meaning non-magical weapons deal virtually no damage).

* **Kadak's Fatal Jump Kick**: In a moment of high-flying martial arts bravado, Kadak attempted a dramatic jump kick directly into the Flesh Golem's chest. Failing his Acrobatics/Jump check against the towering construct, Kadak was caught in mid-air. The Flesh Golem smashed the monk into the frozen earth, crushing him instantly (†).
* **Fendithas Falls**: As the remaining ghouls and shadows pressed in, the Meat Golem turned its massive fists on Fendithas. Despite desperate defensive maneuvers, Fendithas was caught under a heavy slam attack and slain (†).

With two party members down, Jackson, Quni, Jason, and Wölgarr fought with backs against the burial stones, eventually destroying the Flesh Golem and clearing the undead horde just as Noristuor completed his ritual.

## 4\. 3.0e Mechanics Corner: The Wild Boar &amp; The Gnome Reincarnation

On **6 Uktar**, the battered survivors carried the bodies of Kadak and Fendithas back to Ashabenford to arrange for divine resurrection. With high-level *Raise Dead* or *Resurrection* scrolls beyond their financial reach, the party turned to 3.0e primal nature magic: the **Reincarnate** spell.

### How 3.0e *Reincarnate* Differs from 5e

In D&amp;D 5e, casting *Reincarnate* rolls a d100 on a table consisting strictly of playable humanoid races (Elves, Dwarves, Halflings, Dragonborn, etc.). In D&amp;D 3.0e, however, the druidic *Reincarnate* table was far wilder—it included badgers, wolverines, black bears, and wild boars!

### The Results at Ashabenford

When the nature magic washed over the fallen heroes:

1. **Fendithas** was reincarnated as a **Wild Boar** (*villisika*)! Retaining his mental acuity and spellcasting focus, Fendithas found himself trotting on four hooves before heading off into the forest.
2. **Kadak** was reincarnated as a **Gnome**! The formerly tall monk found himself standing barely three feet tall, adjusting to his new small stature before departing into the wilderness.

## 5\. The Tethyamar Intelligence

Despite the tragic loss and bizarre reincarnations, Noristuor's ritual at the Barrow Fields was a complete success. In exchange for their protection and the components, Noristuor provided the party with the exact tactical intelligence needed to enter the **Mines of Tethyamar**:

* **Entrance Options**: The mines can be breached either through a hidden Underdark passage or directly through the fortified main surface entrance.
* **The Objectives**: Noristuor confirmed that deep within the mines lie both the legendary blade **Sargathuld** and the skeletal remains of the ancient dwarven hero **Borlin**.

With this vital map in hand, our adventurers prepare to take their next major step: delving into the dark subterranean depths of Tethyamar in Episode 3!
