Welcome back to the campaign companion blog! Episode 2 marks one of the most action-packed and lore-rich chapters of our early Dalelands campaign. While our adventurers initially set out from Ashabenford on the road toward Shadowdale, their journey quickly escalated into a multi-stage expedition involving ancient dragon remains, high-stakes monster hunting, planar negotiations, a terrifying midnight construct fight, and a pair of unforgettable character reincarnations.

Drawing directly from campaign records across late 2019 and early 2020 (`Campaign notes 2019-11-25`, `2020-01-07`, and `2020-01-13`), this companion breakdown covers the complete, unedited narrative arc of Episode 2—including the full breakdown of Noristuor's three monster contracts and the river hunt for the Sea Lion.

---

## 1\. The Lair in Spiderhaunt Woods: Dragon Bones, Resin Quests, and Psionic Visions

As recorded in `Campaign notes 2019-11-25`, the four-day march from Ashabenford toward Shadowdale began with two distinct side preparations:

* **Blibin's Resin Request**: Before leaving town, Fendithas visited the local gem merchant, **Blibin**, who asked the party to harvest rare tree resin from the treacherous Spiderhaunt Woods.
* **The Druid Warning**: Quni scouted ahead into the forest to meet with a local druid circle, receiving a stark warning about a dangerous creature lurking in the woods near their planned travel path.

On the third day of winter travel, Quni’s tracking skills led the party to a pitch-black, unnatural cavern opening carved into a rocky ridge. Venturing into the gloom, the adventurers discovered the massive, fossilized skeleton of an ancient green dragon embedded directly into the cavern wall, surrounded by the scattered remains of humanoid warriors.

### The Sword in the Dragon's Neck &amp; The Psionic Flashback

Protruding from the dragon's neck bones was a massive, magical dwarven blade. As Fendithas approached the blade, lingering **psionic energy**—preserved in the stone for centuries—triggered a vivid mental vision:

* Fendithas witnessed **Kesten**, a legendary dwarf warrior from the famous adventurer band *Grimblade*, locked in life-or-death combat with the dragon.
* The vision culminated as a psionic dwarf priest channeled raw mental force to pin the green dragon **Urna** against the rock face, allowing Kesten to deliver the fatal strike at the cost of his own life.

This discovery provided physical proof that the green dragon Urna had indeed been slain here by Grimblade, linking the rumors in Ashabenford directly to the lost blade **Sargathuld**.

## 2\. Deciphering the Runes &amp; Noristuor's Three Beast Contracts

Returning to Ashabenford with their findings, the party re-examined the cryptic dwarven statuette recovered in Episode 1 (`Campaign notes 2019-11-25` &amp; `2020-01-07`). The scholar Arvien completed her translation of the High Dwarven runes, confirming that the statuette depicted **Fyrfar Smokebeard**—the ancient master dwarven smith who forged Sargathuld specifically to slay orcs.

To locate where Sargathuld and the remains of Borlin (Wölgarr's father) were resting within the lost **Mines of Tethyamar**, the party consulted the local broker and sage **Noristuor** at Holfast's Inn. Noristuor agreed to provide safe entry coordinates into Tethyamar, but he refused standard gold coins. Instead, he demanded payment in raw magical components: specifically, **the eye of a magical beast** and **pure magical residuum**.

### Evaluating Noristuor's Three Options

Noristuor presented the party with three distinct local rumors regarding magical beasts whose harvestable organs could satisfy his initial requirement:

1. **Option 1: The River Sea Lion (Local Waterways)**
&nbsp;&nbsp;* *Target*: A amphibious, razor-clawed predator lurking along the local riverbank near Ashabenford.
&nbsp;&nbsp;* *Assessment*: Closest proximity to town, but high tactical risk due to aquatic ambush capabilities, heavy natural armor, and multi-attack Rend abilities.
2. **Option 2: The Orc Shaman of Gruumsh (The Standing Stone)**
&nbsp;&nbsp;* *Target*: An elite orc dark-caster encamped two days' travel north near the ancient landmark known as the Standing Stone.
&nbsp;&nbsp;* *Assessment*: Required a multi-day overland trek through hostile territory and guaranteed a fight against a fortified orc warband protecting their spellcasting leader.
3. **Option 3: The Planar Ravid (Abandoned Wizard's Tower)**
&nbsp;&nbsp;* *Target*: A strange, hovering positive-energy creature haunting a ruined wizard's tower to the south.
&nbsp;&nbsp;* *Assessment*: Highly unpredictable due to the Ravid's aura which spontaneously animates inanimate objects, though potentially open to non-violent negotiation if approached carefully.

After weighing the choices, the party elected to hunt the **River Sea Lion** first to secure the beast's eye, while planning a secondary leg to handle the Ravid at the wizard's tower for the required magical residuum.

## 3\. The Sea Lion River Hunt &amp; The Ravid Negotiation

Moving stealthily down to the riverbanks outside Ashabenford (`Campaign notes 2020-01-07`), Quni and her wolf companion Nyx tracked the wet, clawed prints of the Sea Lion along the muddy shore.

### The Riverbank Ambush

* **The Strike**: As the massive creature surged out of the murky water attempting a surprise leap, Jackson and Wölgarr moved in to form a shield wall, absorbing the beast's initial charge.
* **Tactical Execution**: Quni positioned Nyx for a 3.0e Flanking bonus while Fendithas channeled psionic combat boosts. Despite the Sea Lion's high natural armor and fierce claw-claw-bite multi-attack routine, the party's tight coordination brought the beast down before it could drag anyone into deep water.
* **Harvesting the Organ**: Using fine medical tools, Fendithas and Quni successfully harvested the Sea Lion's intact eyes to deliver to Noristuor.

### The Ravid Negotiation at the Wizard's Tower

To fulfill Noristuor's second requirement (magical residuum by 29 Marpenoth), Fendithas traveled south to the abandoned wizard's tower. As Fendithas entered, stools, spellbooks, and torches began hovering in mid-air due to the Ravid's passive positive-energy aura. Rather than attacking, Fendithas initiated telepathic communication, successfully negotiating an exchange of minor magical trinkets for the required residuum without triggering a deadly construct brawl.

## 4\. Midnight at the Barrow Fields: The Meat Golem &amp; Reincarnation

With both components in hand, the party escorted Noristuor to the ancient **Barrow Fields** burial ground on **3 Uktar** to conduct his midnight ritual (`Campaign notes 2020-01-13`).

As midnight struck, the ritual unleashed dark necrotic feedback, drawing ghouls, shadows, and a horrific, stitched-together **Meat/Flesh Golem** into the graveyard.

### High-Stakes Combat &amp; Tragic Losses

* **Kadak's Fatal Jump Kick**: Kadak, the Githzerai Monk, attempted a high-flying martial arts jump kick against the Meat Golem. In 3.0e rules, Flesh Golems possessed heavy Damage Reduction (**DR 10/+1**). Kadak's unweaponized strike failed to bypass the construct's DR, and the Golem countered with a devastating slam, crushing Kadak to death (†)!
* **Fendithas Falls**: Fendithas stepped up to channel psionic powers against the construct but was caught under a second crushing blow, perishing on the field (†).
* **Rallying Victory**: Jackson, Quni, Jason, and Wölgarr focused their attacks and successfully destroyed the Meat Golem and cleared the remaining undead.

### The 3.0e Wild Reincarnation Table (6 Uktar)

Carrying their fallen comrades back to Ashabenford, the survivors paid for *Reincarnate* spells on 6 Uktar. Unlike 5e's humanoid-only table, D&amp;D 3.0e *Reincarnate* included random animals and exotic races:

* **Fendithas** rolled on the wild nature table and reincarnated as a **Wild Boar** (*villisika*), choosing to retreat into the deep forest to embrace her new feral form!
* **Kadak** rolled on the table and reincarnated as a **Gnome**, leaving his monk path behind to seek new adventures elsewhere!

With the ritual completed, Noristuor fulfilled his bargain—handing over the precise maps detailing both the Underdark tunnels and the front door entrance to the Mines of Tethyamar.
