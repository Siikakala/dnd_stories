# Episode 6: The Forge of Roldilar &amp; The Four-Headed Dragon

## Overview &amp; Table Vibe

High-level dwarven mythology, wish spell mechanics, and multi-headed draconic boss fights.

## Key Narrative Beats

* **Nuthbok's Castle &amp; Janni Wishes**: Storming Nuthbok's fortress, freeing Sal'kin, and gaining three *Wish* spells.
* **Meeting Thorsamson**: Deep in Grimmerfang Mountain, meeting Fyrfar Smokebeard preserved as a dwarven lich (Thorsamson).
* **Re-forging Sarghathuld**: Empowering *Sarghathuld* at the sacred adamantium forge.
* **Iyrauroth the Mutant Dragon**: Defeating Iyrauroth, a terrifying four-headed black dragon.

## 3.0e Rules &amp; Tactical Mechanics

* **Handling Wish Spells**: DM guidelines and mechanics for resolving open-ended wishes in 3.0e.
* **Dwarven Lichdom**: Unique template features of dwarven undead preservers.

## Character Spotlights

* **Wölgarr**: Taking up the mantle of Roldilar's restorer with the empowered *Sarghathuld*.
