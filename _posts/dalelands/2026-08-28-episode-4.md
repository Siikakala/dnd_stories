Welcome back to the campaign companion blog! Episode 4 marks a major transition in our Dalelands D&amp;D 3.0e campaign, bridging the party's exploration of the lost dwarven realm of Tethyamar with their initial dive into the haunted subterranean shrine beneath the Standing Stone.

Drawing directly from campaign notes across March 2020 (`Campaign notes 2020-03-10`, `2020-03-17`, `2020-03-24`, and `2020-03-26`), this companion breakdown corrects the precise chronological sequence of events—from the discovery of the legendary blade **Sargathuld** to the initial, temporary destruction of the Lich's physical body.

---

## 1\. The Workshop Floor of Tethyamar: Borlin's Rest and Sargathuld

As recorded in `Campaign notes 2020-03-17`, the party descended into the lower Workshop Floor of the Mines of Tethyamar. Navigating ancient stone avenues littered with dwarven remnants, Wölgarr led the adventurers to the final resting place of his father, **Borlin**.

### Finding Sargathuld

Before encountering any Zhentarim forces on the Workshop Floor, the party discovered Borlin's skeleton slumped against an ancient anvil. Clutched tightly in Borlin's bony hands was **Sargathuld**—the legendary dwarven blade forged by Fyrfar Smokebeard specifically to slay dark forces and orcs.

* **Wölgarr Reclaims the Blade**: Wölgarr retrieved Sargathuld from his father's grasp, fulfilling a primary character milestone.
* **Soul Damage**: Examination of Borlin's remains revealed ominous necrotic scarring, indicating that his soul had been consumed or severely damaged by the dark entities that overran Tethyamar.

---

## 2\. Breaching the Adamantine Door: Kara Chermos and Arvien's End

After resting for a full day in the workshop area to recover spells and prepare for heavy combat, the heroes approached a massive adamantine door situated directly behind Borlin's resting place.

### The Zhentarim Ambush &amp; Liberation

* **Breaching the Sanctum**: Forcing open the adamantine portal, the party confronted Zhentarim Commander **Kara Chermos**, the traitorous scholar **Arvien**, and a heavy detachment of Zhentarim guards.
* **Decisive Battle**: Wölgarr channeling Sargathuld alongside Jackson's raw physical power, Quni's battlefield control, and Dush's ferocious charges overwhelmed the Zhentarim defenders, slaying Kara Chermos and ending Arvien's treachery once and for all.
* **Rescuing the Slaves**: Following the battle, the party freed dozens of enslaved human miners forced into hard labor by the Zhents, securing the Workshop Floor and recovering extensive dwarven relics.

---

## 3\. Downtime &amp; Salvage: The 20-Day Return to Ashabenford

Following their victory at Tethyamar, the party undertook a 20-day march back to Ashabenford, escorting the freed miners to safety (`Campaign notes 2020-03-24`).

* **10 Nightal Salvage Sale**: Arriving in Ashabenford, the party met with **Noristuor** at Holfast's Inn to execute their salvage contract, liquidating ancient dwarven trade goods, ingots, and minor relics for substantial gold.
* **The Standing Stone Contract (13 Nightal)**: While enjoying downtime at the inn, Jason negotiated a contract from Holfast to investigate unholy Gruumsh Shaman activity and strange magical disturbances near the ancient landmark known as the **Standing Stone**.

---

## 4\. Subterranean Standing Stone: Wasps, Pitfalls, and the Elven Pantheon

Arriving at the Standing Stone, Dush accidentally stepped on an ancient arrow carving, vanishing through an invisible portal into a subterranean dungeon complex beneath the monument. The rest of the party quickly followed.

### Room 1: The Deity Drawing &amp; Wasp Attack

* Exploring the first chamber, the party discovered the journal of Fastren (dated 1352 DR) and a detailed wall drawing depicting an **Illithid deity**.
* **Kevan's Psychic Pain**: Kevan inspected the drawing and took severe psychic feedback. As Dush and Jackson pulled Kevan back, a swarm of **Giant Wasps** descended from the ceiling, which the party quickly slaughtered.

### Room 2: Pitfalls &amp; Charging Minotaur

* Moving down a narrow, 10-foot-wide limestone corridor, Quni spotted concealed **pitfall traps** built into the floor.
* As the party carefully bypassed the traps, a massive **Minotaur** wielding a heavy greataxe charged around the corner. Using tight corridor positioning, Jackson and Dush pinned the beast and cut it down.

### Room 3: The Four Elven Statues Puzzle (`2020-03-26`)

Advancing to a sealed stone hall, the party encountered four statues representing the Elven Pantheon surrounding an altar containing colored potions:

1. **Corellon Larethian** (Leader of the Pantheon)
2. **Angharradh** (Triple Goddess of Wisdom &amp; Growth)
3. **Shevarash** (Deity of Vengeance)
4. **Deep Sashelash** (Deity of the Sea)

By correctly analyzing the divine domain riddles and placing the corresponding colored potions before each statue, the magical seal on the inner sanctum dissolved. A second Minotaur guarding the inner threshold charged out but was immediately dispatched by the party.

---

## 5\. The Climax of Episode 4: Slaying the Lich's Physical Body

Unsealing the inner chamber, the party breached the sanctum where a powerful **Lich** (Fastren, Holfast's former adventuring companion who had fallen to corruption) hovered over an unholy altar.

* **High-Stakes Spell Combat**: The Lich unleashed high-tier necrotic magic, but Wölgarr's divine protection and Kevan's holy aura shielded the party.
* **Temporary Victory**: Dush and Jackson charged the altar, combining martial strikes and divine smites to shatter the Lich's physical body.
* **The Cliffhanger**: As the Lich's physical form crumbled into dust, its spectral essence laughed maniacally as it faded into the Ethereal Plane. Episode 4 ends here inside the Standing Stone dungeon—with the party realizing the dungeon continues deeper and that the Lich's phylactery remains hidden on another plane!
