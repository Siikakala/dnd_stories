# Episode 3: The Tower of Shadows &amp; Daggerfall

## Overview &amp; Table Vibe

Focusing on political liberation, shade corruption, and divine callings in Daggerdale.

## Key Narrative Beats

* **Ambushing Zhentarim Patrols**: Intercepting Zhent riders who carry dossiers tracking the party's movements.
* **Kevan's Divine Calling**: Meeting Kevan, who transforms from a wine-loving bard into a devout Paladin of Sune riding his griffon Tipi.
* **Gothyl's Tower of Shadows**: Rescuing Randal from a shade-possessed tower controlled by the witch Gothyl.
* **Liberating Daggerfall**: Deposing puppet ruler Malyk and dismantling Kara Chermos's Zhentarim network.

## 3.0e Rules &amp; Tactical Mechanics

* **Touch AC &amp; Flat-Footed AC**: Explaining the three distinct AC values in 3.0e and how incorporeal shade attacks bypass physical armor.
* **Golem Immunity**: Detailed mechanics of magic immunity on golem constructs.

## Character Spotlights

* **Kevan**: Emerging as the party's moral compass and divine champion of Sune.
* **Randal**: The liberated leader of Daggerdale rallying his people against Zhent oppressors.
