Welcome back to the campaign companion blog! Following the chaotic aftermath of the Barrow Fields and the wild *Reincarnation* rolls in Episode 2, our campaign moves north from Ashabenford toward Shadowdale and Daggerdale. Episode 3 marks a major turning point in the Dalelands campaign: the introduction of two new party members, high-stakes wilderness combat in deep snow, and the discovery of a Zhentarim conspiracy reaching directly into the Mines of Tethyamar.

Drawing directly from campaign records across early 2020 (`Campaign notes 2020-01-20`, `2020-03-02`, and `2020-03-10`), this companion breakdown walks through how tavern meetings, giant ambushes, and haunted wizard towers lay the groundwork for our party's descent into Tethyamar.

---

## 1\. New Blood in Ashabenford: Dush and Kevan Join the Party

As noted in the session records from **20 January 2020**, the party returned to Ashabenford to conclude business with the broker **Noristuor**. In addition to arranging guides and information regarding Tethyamar, Noristuor established an ongoing deal to buy dwarven trade goods and relics recovered by the party during their upcoming mine exploration.

While gathering at the local tavern in Ashabenford, the party welcomed two new adventurers to replace the fallen from the Barrow Fields:

* **Dush (played by Tsuru)**: A wild, unpredictable **Frenzying Barbarian**. Tsuru created Dush after his previous character, Fendithas, perished against the Meat Golem and reincarnated as a wild boar before retreating into the wilderness. Dush brings raw, unstoppable frontline aggression to the party.
* **Kevan (played by Haukka)**: A charming **Paladin of Sune**. Haukka created Kevan to replace Kadak, his Githzerai monk who was crushed by the Meat Golem in Episode 2 and reincarnated as a Gnome before departing the group. Kevan began his career as an unsuccessful bard with a fondness for wine, but after discovering the divine beauty and grace of Sune, he took up the oath of a Paladin.

The simultaneous arrival of Dush and Kevan brought an immediate shift in party dynamics—balancing Dush's wild, impulsive battle rage with Kevan's suave, divine charisma alongside Jackson's martial discipline, Quni's druidic wilderness senses, Jason's rogue pragmatism, and Wölgarr's dwarven devotion.

---

## 2\. Into the Uktar Snow: Gnoll Traps and the Snow-Covered Hill Giant

Setting out on the road north during the cold month of Uktar, the party faced harsh winter conditions. On the third day of travel, Quni’s wilderness senses picked up the sound of a gnoll war band lurking ahead in the heavy snow.

* **Flanking the Gnolls**: Rather than walking into an ambush, Jackson, Quni, Kevan, and Dush maneuvered around the enemy position to strike the gnolls from behind.
* **The Hill Giant Surprise**: As the skirmish began, the ground erupted—a massive **Hill Giant** concealed beneath a heavy snowbank burst forth to attack the party!
* **Tactical Resolution**: Combining Quni's animal companion tactics with Jackson’s physical might, Kevan's divine support, and Dush’s terrifying charge, the party brought down the giant and slaughtered the gnolls before they could regroup.

Upon reaching **Shadowdale on 10 Uktar**, Dush celebrated their victory by throwing a raucous, tavern-wide party (*kunnolliset bileet*), establishing himself as the life of the tavern in every town they visit.

---

## 3\. The Road to Dagger Falls: Intercepting the Zhentarim Patrol &amp; The Outdated Intel

Moving further north toward **Dagger Falls** on **2 March 2020**, the party intercepted a mounted patrol of **Zhentarim riders**. The Zhents had been actively monitoring travel routes through the Dalelands to secure their influence over Daggerdale.

The party engaged the Zhent patrol in swift, decisive combat. Searching the fallen Zhent captain, Jason recovered a detailed tactical journal containing:

1. **Target Profiles &amp; Outdated Spy Intel**: The journal contained explicit written descriptions of Jackson, Quni, Jason, Kevan, **Kadak**, and Wölgarr. The inclusion of **Kadak**—who had died, reincarnated as a gnome, and departed the party back in Episode 2—revealed that Zhentarim intelligence was relying on spy reports compiled earlier in the campaign (up through 18 Uktar). Their network had captured the party's original line-up, but was completely unaware that Kadak had departed and that Dush had joined!
2. **Frustration with Randal Morn**: Curses and complaints regarding local freedom fighter **Randal Morn**, whose constant guerrilla raids had severely disrupted Zhentarim supply lines and outposts.

---

## 4\. Malyk's Haunted Tower: The Gem Pedestals and Shadow Spirits

Following clues from the Zhent journal, the party located an isolated, ruined tower near Daggerdale. Approaching the structure, Quni and Jackson immediately detected the heavy, oppressive stench of **necrotic magic**.

Upon entering the main chamber, the adventurers found an illusory image depicting a man resembling Randal Morn, positioned in the center of six stone pedestals. Each pedestal held a glowing, infused gem.

* **Dush's Impulsive Smite**: Before Jason could examine the pedestals for mechanical traps or arcana runes, Dush charged forward and smashed one of the gems with his weapon!
* **The Necrotic Trap**: Destroying the gem instantly shattered the illusion, releasing a horde of shadowy, ethereal spirits while the ghostly cackle of an unseen witch echoed through the tower chamber.
* **Cleansing the Chamber**: Wölgarr’s divine turning power, combined with Kevan’s holy smites and Jackson’s relentless blades, destroyed the shadow spirits and purged the necrotic corruption from the room.

---

## 5\. Unmasking the Conspiracy: Malyk's Spellbook and Arvien's Treachery

Clearing the upper chambers of the tower yielded the personal belongings and spellbook of **Malyk**—Randal Morn's cousin, who had previously served as the Zhentarim's puppet ruler over Daggerdale before being overthrown.

Tucked within Malyk's spellbook was critical correspondence between Malyk and **Kara Chermos**, a high-ranking Zhentarim commander:

* **Targeting Tethyamar**: The letters revealed that the Zhentarim were mounting a secret expedition into the Mines of Tethyamar to seize its legendary dwarven mithril and ancient artifacts.
* **Arvien's Role**: The correspondence explicitly confirmed that the Zhents had been unable to locate the entrance to Tethyamar until **18 Uktar**, when the scholar **Arvien**—acting as a Zhentarim spy in Ashabenford—successfully spied the coordinates and delivered them to Kara Chermos!

This discovery confirmed that the party's race to Tethyamar was no longer just an exploration quest, but a high-stakes military race against the Zhentarim.

---

## 6\. Underdark Reconnaissance (10 March 2020)

Armed with Malyk's documents, the party met with Randal Morn to share their findings regarding Kara Chermos and the Zhentarim advance force inside Tethyamar.

Recognizing the threat, Randal Morn agreed to dispatch his experienced wilderness scouts down through subterranean Underdark passages to survey the lower entrances of the mines (`Campaign notes 2020-03-10`). This scouting report would provide the party with tactical intelligence before they launched their direct assault into Tethyamar.

---
