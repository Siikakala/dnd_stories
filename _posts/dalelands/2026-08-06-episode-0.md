Welcome to **Episode 0** of our campaign podcast companion blog! Whether you are a tabletop veteran or a D&amp;D 5th Edition player curious about how older editions operated in high-level play, this series breaks down an epic tabletop campaign from Level 1 all the way to Level 24.

## 1\. Setting the Stage: The Dalelands in DR 1372 &amp; The 3.0e Framework

Our story begins on **1\. Marpenoth, 1372 DR (Dale Reckoning)** in the classic **Dalelands** region of the Forgotten Realms. When planning this campaign, the Dungeon Master evaluated which ruleset would best accommodate a long-term saga spanning mortal origins to epic demigod power.

### Why D&amp;D 3rd Edition (3.0e)?

For players familiar with 5th Edition's "bounded accuracy" system—where stats, attack bonuses, and Armor Class remain within relatively tight numerical limits throughout 20 levels—3rd Edition offers a starkly different experience.

* **Epic Progression Beyond Level 20**: In D&amp;D 3.0e, level scaling extends smoothly into epic levels through dedicated rules frameworks, allowing characters to attain Level 24 and beyond with massive stat bonuses, epic feats, and reality-shaping magic.
* **Granular Customization**: 3.0e relies heavily on intricate skill point allocation, specific saving throw categories (Fortitude, Reflex, Will), and deep feat chains.
* **High-Stakes Numbers**: Armor Class, saving throws, and skill DCs scale well into the 30s and 40s as characters advance into high tier and epic gameplay.

While 5e streamlines mechanics for quick play, 3.0e provided our group with the exact mechanical crunch required to track high-powered progression over years of play.

## 2\. Table Dynamics, "Finglish", and Macro-Campaign Scale

The gaming group consists of **seven total participants**: Dungeon Master **Muhrri** (a veteran with 20 years of DMing experience who began playing at age 13) and six players based primarily in Tampere, Finland.

&gt; **Table Atmosphere**: The gaming sessions featured a natural blend of Finnish narrative dialogue and English D&amp;D rule mechanics, reflecting standard Finnish tabletop culture where rulebooks exist primarily in English.

### Key Aspects of Our Table Culture

* **Finglish Dialogue**: In Finnish gaming culture, standard play naturally blends Finnish narrative conversation with English rule terminology. Players regularly exchange phrases like *"Heittäkää initiativee"* (Roll initiative) or *"Forttisave DC on 20"* (Fortitude save DC is 20).
* **Retro CRPG &amp; Lore References**: Table banter frequently draws on classic computer role-playing games such as *Baldur's Gate*, *Baldur's Gate II*, and *Icewind Dale*, alongside the fantasy novels of R. A. Salvatore. Inside jokes abound—such as enemies "gibbing" into five equal blood chunks on a critical hit.
* **Transition to Remote Play**: While the campaign originally convened in-person, the group transitioned to online play using **Roll20** for virtual tabletop maps and **Discord** for voice communications during the spring of 2020.
* **Multi-Party World Continuity**: The Dalelands campaign existed within a larger macro-campaign run by the DM featuring three distinct adventuring groups operating simultaneously in the same world:
  1. **Our Main Party** (residing in Tampere).
  2. **The Valkeakoski Group** (featuring the brother of players Jackson and Jason).
  3. **A British Solo Player** (playing the character Sofia, Queen Regent of Hillsfar, online).

## 3\. Core House Rules: The Legendary "Säkäheitto" (Scale of Justice)

To balance 3.0e's strict mechanical framework with player creativity, DM Muhrri instituted a cornerstone house rule known as the **Säkäheitto** (translated as the *"luck roll"* or *"gamble roll"*).

| House Rule Component | Mechanical Execution | Narrative Outcome |
| ---------------------| -------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Creative Player Actions** | Player rolls **d20** vs DM rolls **d20** ("Scale of Justice") | When a player attempts an imaginative maneuver not covered by formal rules, contested d20 rolls dictate success. Close rolls result in gray-area interpretations. |
| **Dynamic Random Encounters** | DM rolls d20 against Player's d20 | High player roll vs Low DM roll yields positive encounters or beneficial boons. Low player roll vs High DM roll triggers unusually dangerous or lethal threats. |

This mechanic kept non-standard actions swift and dramatic without requiring complex rule-lookup pauses during sessions.

## 4\. The Starting Roster: Level 1 Adventurers

Every epic campaign begins with humble beginnings. Below is the initial starting party at Level 1, detailing the players and their original character concepts:

### Player &amp; Character Roster Overview

* **Siika** (She/Her | Goth &amp; Modern Witch | DM's Cousin | \~15 Yrs Experience)
  * **Starting Character**: **Quni** — A Halfling Druid hailing from Chondalwood, bound to Mielikki, and accompanied by her loyal wolf companion **Nyx**. *(Note: Siika later played Tiefling Sorceress Zelene and Gnome Artificer Charissa as the campaign progressed).*
* **Jenni** (She/Her | DM's Wife | Campaign Scribe | \~15 Yrs Experience)
  * **Starting Character**: **Jackson** — A human Fighter from a rural farming family. Jackson is a loyal mother's boy who sends the majority of his adventuring earnings home to his mother Meredith.
* **Calzu** (He/Him | Tactical Mind | 15+ Yrs Experience)
  * **Starting Character**: **Jason** — A human Rogue and jack-of-all-trades. Jackson's brother, Jason is driven by commerce and wealth accumulation, constantly re-investing gold into new business ventures while leaving heavy frontline combat to his brother.
* **Tsuru** (He/Him)
  * **Starting Character**: **Fendithas** — A Halfling Psion mastering mental disciplines. *(Tsuru also played the barbarian Dush during middle levels before Fendithas returned).*
* **Haukka** (He/Him | Second Newest Player)
  * **Starting Character**: **Kadak** — A Githzerai Monk skilled in the art of the three-piece staff. *(Haukka later played Kevan, a wine-loving Bard who underwent a holy awakening to become a Paladin of Sune).*
* **Smoggis** (He/Him | Newest Player)
  * **Starting Character**: **Wölgarr** — A dedicated Dwarf Cleric bringing divine power and dwarven resilience to the party.
